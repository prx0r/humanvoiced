# HumanVoiced — the global voice marketplace for ordinary people

Anyone with a phone, a voice and an internet connection should be able to
create a portfolio that AI agents anywhere can discover and hire. A person
in Cambodia records a 30-second English sample plus an optional Khmer
sample, and gets hired to narrate, dub or perform — no agent, studio,
pro mic or acting career needed.

## Principles

- Joining is free everywhere (no pay-to-work appearance). USD reference
  pricing; display local equivalents; pay out in supported local currency
  or crypto. Same work, same USD value, wherever the narrator lives.
- Profiles are global from day one; paid bookings open country by country
  only where a verified, legal payout route exists. Discoverable ≠ bookable.
- Accent is sound, not nationality or skill proof. No caricature casting;
  workers set content boundaries. Adults only at launch. No cloning without
  separate agreement.
- Payout economics: 100% of fee to worker; platform/donor funds cover
  processor minimums ($1.50+ per international payout makes per-clip
  transfers senseless → minimum withdrawals + combined payouts).
- Providers: Payoneer (190+ countries), Wise Platform (160+/40+),
  Stripe Global Payouts (rails vary), x402 agent checkout, MSVault QU
  prototype. Each corridor verified with real test transactions before
  marking a country bookable. Cambodia crypto: NBC regulation → verify first.

## Wedge (international)

Recurring YouTube narration + native dubbing (YouTube multilingual audio
tracks work on new AND existing videos) + recurring bilingual channel
voices. First corridors to verify; pilot markets Cambodia/India/
Philippines for recruitment, payments only where checks pass.

## Rollout (30 days)

1. Global registration (light uploads, recovery on bad networks).
2. First 30 diverse voices (reachable people first).
3. 2–3 verified payout corridors (real test transactions).
4. Creator contracts → measure paid work delivered, not countries listed.
5. Languages follow paid requests; funded WANTED ads recruit supply.

Metric: narrators earning first dollar in 30 days; creators rebooking.
Moat: portable verified work history (languages, jobs, clients, credits,
0% commission) — location-independent proof of reliable human work.
