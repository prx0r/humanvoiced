# Demand map (researched Oct 2026)

## Faceless YouTube (primary wedge, validated)

Automation teams hire per-video VO at $20–60 ($15–50 range) for 2–4
videos/week. High-CPM niches: finance/investing ($15–40), history,
horror/storytelling, science. Pain points we solve: robotic AI voices get
swiped in 3 seconds; consistency across uploads; QC without managing
freelancers. Entry: $10–11/10min human narration vs $5–22/mo ElevenLabs —
sell reliability + repeatability, not cheapness. Shorts for discovery,
8–15min long-form for revenue; our contracts cover both.

## Film/TV (exposed volume work, not leads)

Leads are safe (audiences pay for people). Exposed: background/crowd,
DUBBING & LOCALISATION, pick-up lines, game character voices, commercials.
SAG-AFTRA 2026 terms: consent per project, minimum compensation, synthetics
stay the exception; UK personality right possibly coming. Our consent-first,
per-contract, compensated model is aligned with where regulation is going —
position as the compliant way to hire human voices, with paper trails
(studio buyers need this). No celeb impersonations, ever.

## Dubbing (streamers depend on it)

Netflix-grade demand (Squid Game/Lupin scale); AI dubbing quality mixed;
European voice actors organizing (TouchePasMaVF); AI firms themselves say
humans are key for quality. Our dub package (approved script + WAV + SRT +
alignment) plus reviewer→narrator chaining is exactly the workflow.

## Agent API implications

Agents need: role breakdowns with sides (audition excerpts), multi-role
cast lists in one call, stage/director notes attached to contracts,
character-voice profiles distinct from natural voice, pick-up line orders
(small corrections flow already exists), dub packages with timecodes.
