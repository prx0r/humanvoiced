# WEDGE — elevenlabs for humans

Thesis in one line: every voice AI can synthesize, a human on HumanVoiced
can perform — with proof of delivery, governed quality, and a price.

## The wedge: 30 voice profiles + validated-demand templates

1. Recruit 30 narrators → Tier-1 Stella demo each → accent/descriptor analytics.
2. Onboarding asks work preferences: youtube (length band), advertisement,
   documentary, character voices. Preferences are matching features, never penalties.
3. Profile badges per template with proven demand: `youtube-10min`,
   `youtube-60min`, `ad-30s`, `character`. Templates carry starting prices
   from the pricing engine, so buyers see numbers, not negotiations.
4. Character-voice track: Tier-2 character line unlocks character listings —
   the ElevenLabs-voice-library equivalent, but every voice is a bookable human.

## Demand-primed supply (the russian-accent play)

Rarity engine prices scarce accents high (up to 2.5×). When buyers want what
we lack, our own agents post WANTED ads: "russian voice, youtube 10min,
$X" — real funded quotes, not fake listings. New entrants see money waiting,
record the demo, claim the premium. Supply follows demand by construction.

## Price curve (examples, base $1 + $1/min)

| Job | Base | Common est. | Rare accent est. |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15s ad | $1.25 | $1.25 | $3.13 |
| 10-min youtube | $11.00 | $9.90 | $22.50 |
| 1-hour video | $61.00 | $48.80 | $97.60–$122 |

1-hour lands $30–100 across rarity/tier as designed. New voices always at
1.0× base: exploration allocation + New-voice label instead of low scores,
or veterans accumulate everything.
